Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How do I reset my password?

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your email address and we'll send you a reset link. Follow the instructions to create a new password.

Where do I find my saved progress?

Your progress is automatically saved. Go to Dashboard → My Courses to see your completed lessons and progress percentage for each course.

How do I upgrade to coaching?

Navigate to Dashboard → Coaching. Select your preferred package and complete the checkout process. Our team will contact you within 24 hours.

How does Coach Marni AI work?

Coach Marni AI is available 24/7. Click the AI button, ask any dating or confidence question, and get instant personalized advice based on Marni's teachings.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel anytime. Go to Account → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of your billing period.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes! We offer a 15-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, contact support within 15 days for a full refund.
